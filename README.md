# Via Romana

A recumbent bike ride round a ruined Roman town: the Forum and its triumphal arch, the theater, the Colosseum, the road of
tombs, Roman houses, the baths and the aqueduct, every one passed once a loop, with vineyards, olive groves, pine woods and
Mount Vesuvius on the horizon. The bike rides itself; at three forks you look left or right to choose the way, and you can
look around as you ride. Made for students who steer with their head (webcam face tracking, all on your own computer) or a
mouse.

Open index.html from a web server (it needs https or localhost for the webcam). Everything is in this folder: no downloads.

Built with three.js (MIT) and MediaPipe Face Landmarker (Apache 2.0, in ./mediapipe).
